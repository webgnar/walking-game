Tutorial intro: storyboard

A mockup of a skippable intro cutscene for The Ascent, narrated by PROCTOR. Each panel is a scene; each numbered line is one beat (one tap or about four seconds). The backgrounds are real screenshots of the current build on main, so every button PROCTOR points at exists today. Tap a panel to play it.

Notes for review

  • Where it sits: the GDD's first session puts a wolf fight at 0:30 with "no menus". This tour runs about 3 minutes, so I'd split it: cold open + wake-up + the walking rule before the wolf, and the screen tour (scenes 4 to 11) right after the tutorial win, where the starter grant already lands. In the real build each scene should point at the live screen, not a screenshot.
  • Skippable: a Skip button on every scene; Settings could get "Replay intro".
  • Placeholder art: PROCTOR is the eye-and-bubble from the game. The cold open and era ladder would be comic panels in the final art pass.
  • PROCTOR voice is still open in the GDD (text or voiced). Lines are written to work as either.
